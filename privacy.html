Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

GTA Auto Clipper is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels to upload videos, update their titles and descriptions, and read channel and video statistics.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Video statistics are stored locally. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no data is sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube APIs.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
